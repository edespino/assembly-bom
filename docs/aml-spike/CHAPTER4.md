# Chapter 4, spoken intro

## Say this

Same practice bank. New screen: What the watch found.

In the first minute, nobody caught a criminal. The marks were planted, and the chart only showed them. This chapter, the bank's own watch goes looking, and it does not get to see the marks.

The watch looked for one shape only. Money that leaves and comes back, through a ring of at least five accounts. It did not look for the other two shapes.

It named 10,183 rings. Then the marks were opened, and the score was taken. That is the chart: The score, after the marks were opened. Planted rings found: 3. Planted rings missed: 1. Into one and Out to many, not searched for: 6. Rings the watch named: 10,183. Named, and the marks say no: 10,171.

So. Ten planted schemes. Four are rings, and rings are what the watch was looking for. It found three and missed one. A watch that had seen the marks would have found all four; this one did not see them. The other six are the other two shapes, and the watch was not looking for those. And it named over ten thousand rings of ordinary payments going round in a circle. The answers are known, so the watch can be graded. That is what a practice bank is for.

The sentence to keep: the practice bank's watch found three of the four planted rings, missed one, and named thousands that were not planted.
