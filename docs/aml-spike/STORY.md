# Spoken intro

Read this aloud. Do not read the operator sheet. That file is how you drive the screen. This file is what the listener hears.

Listener: someone who has never seen the host. Introductory session. High level. One example, the small configuration that is running. If they glaze over, stop. Do not add detail to save it.

About one minute. Voice is xAI `sal`.

## Say this

Picture a mid-size bank. Its financial-crime team watches every payment, looking for money laundering.

This is a practice version of that bank. One machine. Nobody's real money is in it.

It carries a hundred thousand ordinary payments. Hidden in them, ten planted laundering schemes. The schemes come in three shapes, and those three names are on the chart in front of you. We call the first one Into One: many payments land in a single account. The second is Out to Many: one account pays many others. The third is Comes Back: the money leaves and returns.

Behind the chart is a database. It holds the accounts, the payments, and a mark on the schemes we planted. The chart is just looking at that database.

One thing to be clear about. Nobody caught a criminal here. We planted the schemes ourselves, and the chart shows where they are. That is the whole of this first minute: a practice bank, with planted laundering schemes, shown back on a chart.

## Do not say

Account numbers. Amounts. Steps. Ports. How you logged in. fsync. That the segments are processes. Fan-in, fan-out, cycle. Product names. Any of that is the driver's sheet, not the talk.

## Afterward, for you

If a listener cannot retell this in one sentence, the story failed. The sentence is: a practice bank, planted laundering schemes, shown back on a screen. If they cannot say that, change the vertical or the dataset. Do not add infrastructure to rescue it.
