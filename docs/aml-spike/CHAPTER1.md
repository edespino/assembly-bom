# Chapter 1, spoken intro

Speak this aloud. Do not speak the operator sheet. The chart is on screen before you start. Speak the titles exactly as they appear; do not rename them.

Listener: someone who heard the one-minute practice-bank story, or could have. No product names. High level.

About one minute. Voice is xAI `sal`.

## Say this

Same practice bank. Same ten planted schemes. One thing has moved.

In the first minute, the payments lived inside the database. Now they also sit in files, in storage outside it. Those files are the bank's history. The database did not pull them in. It looks at them where they sit.

That is the title on the screen: History, where it sits. The chart is titled Planted and ordinary, from the history files. Two slices. Ordinary, and Planted. The planted slice is a sliver: sixty-five payments out of 121,457. Those sixty-five payments are the ten schemes, counted by payment instead of by scheme.

One check. The count from the files and the count from inside the database are the same number. Nothing was lost on the way out, and nothing was added.

The sentence to keep: the practice bank's history sits in files, and the database shows it from where it sits.

## Do not say

Product names. AML. Ports. The dashboard address. The word read. Step. Any calendar date. fsync. How the files got there. Anything from the driver's sheet that is not in the section above.
