# Chapter 4, technical narration

## Say this

You heard the short version: the watch found three of the four planted rings, missed one, and named thousands that were not planted. Here is what the watch is, where it ran, and how it was scored.

The watch is rules in the database. No model. A query that walks payments from account to account and reports when the money comes back to where it started. Its rule has three parts. The shape: money that comes back. The size: a ring of at least five accounts. The window: the ring closes within 15 simulated days, and the simulator has no calendar, so those are its days, not ours.

Where it ran. It ran on the table inside the database, not on the files. That is deliberate, and it is a limit. The file copy does not carry who paid whom. It carries the payments and their marks, but not the payer and the payee, and a ring is nothing but payer and payee, hop by hop. So this search could not run on the files. It ran where the links are.

How it was kept honest. The watch did not see the planted marks while it searched. It named its rings first. Then the marks were opened and laid over the result. The chart title says it in order: The score, after the marks were opened. Four rows.

Rings the watch named: 10,183. Closed loops of five or more accounts inside the window.

Planted rings found: 3. There are ten planted schemes. Four are the comes-back shape. Three of those four sat entirely inside a ring the watch named.

Planted rings missed: 1. The fourth planted ring did not sit inside any named ring. This talk does not say why.

Named, and the marks say no: 10,171. Loops of ordinary payments that are not a planted scheme.

Two things the numbers do not say. The other six planted schemes are the other two shapes, into one and out to many. This watch did not look for them, so it did not miss them. They are simply not on this chart.

What this means. One shape, one rule, and the watch names over ten thousand rings to land on three. In a real bank, every one of those is work for a person. Here the answers are known, so the rule can be graded instead of trusted. That is the use of a practice bank.

What this cut is not. Not a model. Not a detection product. One rule, scored against planted marks, with the score on the screen.

The sentence to keep: the practice bank's watch found three of the four planted rings, missed one, and named thousands that were not planted.
